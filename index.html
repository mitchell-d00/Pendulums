A NOTE ON WHAT YOU’RE SEEING

Pretty paths. Actual physics.

These are spherical pendulums: each mass hangs from a fixed point on a rigid, massless string, free to swing in any horizontal direction. Gravity and initial motion determine the path. We draw each bob’s actual top-down projection on a virtual sand surface—not the path of a bob physically dragging through real sand.

The solver advances the full 3D motion with fixed 1/240-second steps, fourth-order Runge–Kutta, and rod-length/tangent-velocity projection. With damping zero, ideal total energy and vertical angular momentum should stay almost constant, subject to numerical error. Damping removes mechanical energy. Pendulums do not interact or collide; there is no friction from sand.
